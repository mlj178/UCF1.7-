已验证总结（合并版）
===================

来源: codex_diagnostic_bundle + kimi_diagnostic_bundle 合并（以 codex 为准）

一、运行时架构
-------------

1. 进程: 32位 x86, pointerSize=4
2. 调用约定: __cdecl (Windows x86)
3. Frida NativeFunction: 应使用 "mscdecl"
4. Unity: 2020.3.30f1 (IL2CPP)
5. Frida: 17.9.1, frida-tools: 14.8.1

二、地址修正
-----------

6. 4个射击Hook地址错误 (JS+0x1000=IDA)
   - Recoil.GetShootRay: 0x0B195C0
   - WPN_Gun.Damage: 0x0B613D0
   - WPN_Gun.GunShoot: 0x0B624C0
   - WPN_Gun.GunShoot_Logic: 0x0B62170

三、结构体偏移
-------------

7. QVModel.Data 结构体大小 0x38
   - Model: offset 0x00
   - GunFire: offset 0x34

8. QVModel 关键偏移
   - left: offset 0x44
   - right: offset 0x7C

9. CharacterModel.bindQvMdl offset 0xB4

10. Recoil.aimIK offset 0x18 (OffsetModifier版本)

11. AimIK.solver offset 0x1C -> IKSolverAim

12. IKSolver.IKPosition offset 0x8

四、ABI 验证
-----------

13. GetShootRay retBuf 隐藏参数 (最先压栈)

14. Damage Ray inline 栈内联 [ebp+0C]~[ebp+23]

15. Transform 读/写函数 RVA (script.json)
    - get_position_Injected: 0x03F4280
    - set_position_Injected: 0x03F4810
    - get_rotation_Injected: 0x03F4380
    - set_rotation_Injected: 0x03F4870

16. Physics Raycast/SphereCast 存在 (script.json)
    - Raycast: 0x0ABAF40 (Ray, RaycastHit*, float, MethodInfo*)
    - SphereCast: 0x0ABB5C0 (Ray, radius, RaycastHit*, maxDistance, layerMask, MethodInfo*)

五、Camera 函数
-------------

17. Camera.ScreenPointToRay_Injected: 0x0327AD0
    参数: Camera*, Vector2*, int eye, Ray* retBuf, MethodInfo*

18. Camera.WorldToScreenPoint_Injected: 0x0327EE0
    参数: Camera*, Vector3*, int eye, Vector3* retBuf, MethodInfo*

19. Camera.get_main: 0x0328310
20. Camera.get_current: 0x03281C0
21. Camera.get_pixelWidth: 0x0328490
22. Camera.get_pixelHeight: 0x03283F0
